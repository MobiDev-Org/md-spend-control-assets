# MD Spend Control — Privacy Policy

MD Spend Control is an internal MobiDev service.

The service may process business-related information required for subscription and renewal management, including:
- corporate user names and email addresses;
- Google Workspace user identifiers and account status;
- subscription assignments;
- subscription pricing and renewal information;
- notification recipient configuration;
- operational logs and audit records.

The data is used only for internal MobiDev business purposes.

MD Spend Control does not sell personal data and does not provide company data to advertisers.

Google Chat notifications are sent only to users explicitly configured as recipients for a specific subscription.

Access to MD Spend Control data is restricted according to the application's role and scope permissions.

For privacy-related questions, contact the MobiDev administrator responsible for MD Spend Control.
