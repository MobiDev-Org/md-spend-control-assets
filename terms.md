# MD Spend Control — Terms of Use

MD Spend Control is an internal MobiDev tool intended only for authorized company users.

The service is used to manage and monitor company subscriptions, renewal dates, assigned users, and related operational data.

Users must:
- use the service only for legitimate internal business purposes;
- not share access with unauthorized persons;
- not attempt to bypass access controls or security restrictions;
- handle company data in accordance with MobiDev internal policies.

The service is provided for internal use and may be changed, suspended, or updated by MobiDev administrators at any time.

For questions or access issues, contact the MobiDev administrator responsible for MD Spend Control.
